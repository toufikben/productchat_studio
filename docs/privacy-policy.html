ProductChat Studio Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains how ProductChat Studio handles information in the Android application. The application is designed to edit product images on the device.

2. Images and local processing

When you select an image from your device gallery, the application reads the image locally so that you can edit it. The current local editing pipeline processes selected images and generated outputs on the device. It does not upload your product images to our servers. You control whether to share or export an image using Android or another application.

3. Model downloads

The application may download machine-learning model files from the configured public model repository on Hugging Face. These downloads are made over the internet and are used for local processing. Product images are not included in those model-download requests.

4. Purchases

Optional subscriptions and one-time products are provided through Google Play Billing. Google and Google Play process purchase information under their own terms and privacy policy. ProductChat Studio receives purchase events needed to provide the purchased entitlement and keeps only the local purchase state and a one-way fingerprint used to reduce accidental duplicate processing. The application does not store raw purchase tokens in its local entitlement record.

5. Information stored on your device

The application stores settings, free-tier usage state, local entitlement state, brand settings, and bounded History metadata on your device. Generated image files remain local unless you explicitly share, export, or delete them. Deleting a History item or clearing History removes recorded outputs when the files are still available. Uninstalling the application removes its local application data subject to Android system behavior.

6. Permissions and access

The current Android manifest declares internet access for model downloads and the Google Play Billing permission for purchases. The application uses the system image picker to let you select product images from the gallery. It does not require an account, contacts, location, SMS, call logs, or microphone access for the current gallery-based editing flow.

7. Advertising and analytics

The current application does not contain advertisements, does not include an advertising SDK, and does not use advertising ID. The repository does not configure Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or another analytics service. We do not sell your personal information.

8. Children

ProductChat Studio is a general productivity and image-editing application and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your choices

You can decline to select an image, delete local History and generated files from the application, uninstall the application, and control sharing or export through Android. You can also contact us about this policy using the address below.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the application’s data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a new version is published.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: privacy@productchat.app
